tyspace is a public analytics dashboard covering the three properties Tyler Moncrieff runs from Charlotte, North Carolina: an AI search visibility agency, a vacant-land buyer, and an independent horrorcore record label. Same operator, three audiences, one read on what is working.

Open the dashboard → Source on GitHub

What the dashboard tracks

Four numbers describe the whole portfolio: unique visitors, AI citations (times ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude or Google's AI Overviews name a site by name), leads (form submissions, cash-offer requests, label submissions) and an AI visibility score out of 100.

Every view on the page reads the same filter state, so switching a date range, a traffic segment, a device or an AI engine re-renders the KPI cards, the trend line, the volume bars, both donuts, the engine ranking and the content table at the same moment. Views are shareable: the URL keeps the property, range, filters, sort order and page size.

About the data

Every figure in this dashboard is synthetic. The demo dataset is generated in your browser from a seeded random number generator, shaped to look like three real sites — seasonal waves, weekday rhythms, release-day spikes, AI-answer growth and page-level behaviour. It contains no cookies, no tracking pixels and no visitor data, and nothing is sent anywhere. To run it on your own numbers, replace assets/js/data.js with a feed that exposes the same shape (window.TYSPACE) — the charts, cards and table need no changes.
